on transcendence and grief

Real power is bowing down

← the field notes
crystal st. john apr 2026 philosophy

I was sitting in the window seat of an airplane, flying back to Los Angeles, after being home for the funeral of my grandmother.

The light was splashing on the mountains below. I watched as it moved, danced. We live in a time when we get to see the world from this vantage point, a place where even mountains hover beneath us. Yet all I wanted to do was press my cheek against the ground, hug the earth, and say, thank you.

I don't always know what I am thanking God for, but I always know I am being held, carried in some way. And even in the smallness of my human body, looking down upon the massive mountain range, I feel transcendent of all of it.

My spirit cannot be scaled to a size.

It can hug each mountaintop. It can also visit the smallest ant, trailing along through the dirt.

I am not defined by my body. I am not defined by hierarchies or systems that organize this world. I am not defined by success or failure. I am a soul placed in a body, feeling what it is to move through space and time. I am my will, my intentions, and my offering. Everything else is beyond my control.

And for this I know, I am limitless. I can shape my reality each day, even though I cannot shape my circumstances.

✦ ✦ ✦

If I am transcendent, so are you. Titles, or the lack of them, don't define our ability to shape the world, to speak life or death into it. If we are all transcendent, no one is above us, and in that same stroke, no one is beneath us.

To understand one's own transcendence puts within them such confidence, they don't need to prove a thing. Do you need to prove your power, when you know you have it? Or do you feel the weight of it instead. Your words, your actions, your choices. Do they bring poison, or medicine?

If you really believed you are powerful, would you choose revenge? Or do you choose revenge because deep down you feel terribly weak? So weak, you need to defend yourself, because your body is fragile.

But your spirit is transcendent.

It takes great strength to choose to walk in your power. Many don't know their own, so they set out to make you feel powerless.

But true power bows down. True power serves.

True power is gentle. It listens. It sees.

For my grandmother, who knew this long before I did.

Xx Crystal
